Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

The short version

Clean Coffee Index doesn't ask you to sign up, doesn't run analytics or ad trackers, and doesn't sell your information.

What we collect

Nothing directly. The site has no accounts, no forms and no tracking scripts. The "Find my coffee" quiz runs in your browser, and your answers aren't sent to us.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic technical data, such as IP addresses, to keep the service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links. When you click one, the brand or its affiliate network may set a cookie on their own site to credit us if you buy. That cookie is governed by the brand's or network's privacy policy, not ours. Affiliate links never change a brand's proof level or its place on the list.

Featured listings

Brands that buy a featured listing pay through Stripe. Stripe handles all payment details, and we never see or store card numbers. See Stripe's privacy policy for details.

Children

This site isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
