The proof ladder

"Mold-free" is a claim.
A lab report is proof.

No government certification exists for mold-free or low-acid coffee. So every brand here gets one of three proof levels, based on what you can actually check yourself.

● Report published

The brand posts its lab results. You can open them and read the numbers.

● Third-party certified

An outside group tested it and certified it. The detailed numbers stay private.

● Brand says tested

The brand says it tests. We haven't found a public report yet.
